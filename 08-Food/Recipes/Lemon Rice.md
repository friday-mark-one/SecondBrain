## Ingredients
- [[Crystal sona masoori rice]] | 1 cup (raw), cooked fluffy and cooled
- [[Groundnut oil]] | 2 tbsp ([[Sesame oil]] works too)
- [[Mustard]] | 1 tsp
- [[Channa dal]] | 1 tbsp
- [[Urad dal]] | ½ tbsp
- [[Asafotida]] | a pinch
- [[Blanched peanuts]] | 2 tbsp
- [[Cashews]] | 1 tbsp (optional)
- [[Green chilli]] | 2–3, slit
- [[Dry red chilli]] | 1–2
- [[Curry leaves]] | 2 strips
- [[Ginger]] | 1 tsp, grated (optional)
- [[Turmeric]] | ½ tsp
- [[Lemon]] | 1–1½, juiced
- [[Kallu uppu]] | to taste (rock salt)

## Directions
1. Cook the rice so the grains stay separate; spread on a plate, drizzle ½ tsp oil, and cool completely.
2. Heat the oil in a pan. Add mustard seeds and let them splutter.
3. Add channa dal, urad dal, peanuts, and cashews; fry till golden.
4. Add asafoetida, dry red chilli, green chilli, curry leaves, and ginger; sauté 30 s.
5. Add turmeric and rock salt, stir for a few seconds (do not let the turmeric burn), then switch off the heat.
6. Add the cooled rice and mix gently till evenly coated.
7. Squeeze in the lemon juice off the heat and mix. Taste and adjust salt/lemon.
8. Rest 10 min for the flavours to soak in. Serve with poriyal, appalam, or potato fry.

## Notes
- Add lemon only after the pan is off the heat — heat makes it bitter.
- Leftover rice (a day old) gives the best texture.
