## Ingredients
- [[Groundnut oil]] | for sautéing
- [[Dry red chilli]] | 7, round
- [[Urad dal]] | 3 tsp
- [[Toor Dal]] | 1 spoon
- [[Asafotida]] | a pinch
- [[Tamarind paste]] | 1 spoon
- [[Ginger]] | 1 inch (optional)
- [[Coconut]] | 2 cups

## Directions
1. Heat groundnut oil in pan.
2. Add dry red chillies, urad dal, toor dal, asafoetida; sauté till dal turns golden.
3. Transfer the mixture to mixer jar; add tamarind paste, ginger (optional), coconut.
4. Blend everything.
