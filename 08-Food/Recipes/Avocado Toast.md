## Ingredients
- [[Bread]] | 3 slices
- [[Avocado]] | 1 ripe
- [[Pepper powder]] | to taste
- [[Chilli flakes]] | to taste
- [[Sesame seeds]] | roasted, for mixing + garnish
- [[Lemon]] | ¼-½, juiced
- [[Pumpkin seeds]] | to garnish

## Directions
1. Toast the 3 bread slices.
2. Smash the ripe avocado in a bowl.
3. Add ½ tsp salt and mix.
4. Add pepper powder, chilli flakes and roasted sesame seeds.
5. Squeeze in ¼ to ½ lemon.
6. Mix to a creamy consistency.
7. Garnish with more sesame seeds and pumpkin seeds.
