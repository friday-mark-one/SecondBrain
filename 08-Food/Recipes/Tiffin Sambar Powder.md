## Ingredients
- [[Dry red chilli]] | 20g
- [[Byadgi chilli]] | 20g
- [[Coriander seeds]] | 40g
- [[Cumin seeds]] | 20g
- [[Peppercorns]] | 20g
- [[Fenugreek seeds]] | 20g

## Directions
1. Add all ingredients to a mixer jar.
2. Grind to a fine powder.
