## Ingredients
- [[Cashews]] | ¼ cup, soaked in hot water 30 min
- [[Coconut milk]] | ½ cup, thick full-fat canned
- [[Onion]] | 2 medium, finely chopped
- [[Tomato]] | 2 medium, pureed or finely chopped
- [[Ginger garlic paste]] | 1 tbsp (fresh crushed [[Ginger]] + [[Garlic]] is better)
- [[Ghee]] | 2 tbsp total (or oil)
- [[Baby bella mushrooms]] | 1 lb (450 g) white button or cremini, sliced
- [[Methi leaves]] | 2 cups, tightly packed, roughly chopped
- [[Cumin seeds]] | 1 tsp
- [[Turmeric]] | ½ tsp
- [[Kashmiri chilli powder]] | 1½-2 tsp
- [[Coriander powder]] | 1 tbsp
- [[Garam masala]] | 1 tsp
- Salt | to taste

## Directions
1. **Cashew paste:** Drain soaked cashews, blend with 2-3 tbsp water until completely smooth. Set aside.
2. **Sauté methi + mushrooms:** Heat 1 tbsp ghee in a wide pan. Add chopped methi; sauté 2-3 min to cook off raw bitterness. Add sliced mushrooms, cook on high until they release water and brown slightly. Remove and set aside.
3. **Masala base:** Same pan, remaining 1 tbsp ghee. Splutter cumin seeds. Add onions, sauté till golden brown (~7-8 min). Stir in ginger-garlic paste, cook 1 min until fragrant.
4. **Spices + tomatoes:** Add pureed tomatoes, turmeric, Kashmiri chilli, coriander powder and salt. Cook down until oil separates at the sides.
5. **Cashew cream:** Lower heat. Stir in cashew paste, mix well, cook 2 min as gravy thickens. Add ½ cup warm water to adjust consistency.
6. **Coconut milk:** Keep heat LOW — high heat splits the milk. Slowly stir in ½ cup thick coconut milk until fully incorporated and silky.
7. **Simmer + finish:** Return sautéed mushrooms and methi to the pan. Stir, simmer very gently 3-4 min (no rolling boil). Turn off heat, stir in garam masala, serve hot.
