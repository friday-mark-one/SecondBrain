## Ingredients
- [[Water]] | 1 Ikea glass full
- [[Panam Karkandu]] | 1 spoon
- [[Kashayam powder]] | ½ spoon

## Directions
1. Add panam karkandu and kashayam powder to the water.
2. Boil for 7-10 mins.
