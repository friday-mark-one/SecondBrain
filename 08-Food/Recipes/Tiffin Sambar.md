## Ingredients
- [[Toor Dal]] | 50g
- [[Moong dal]] | 50g
- [[Pearl onions]] | 10-12
- [[Drumstick]] | ~8 pieces
- [[Turmeric]] | ½ tsp
- [[Ghee]] | 1 tbsp
- [[Tomato]] | 1, cut
- [[Tamarind paste]] | ½ spoon (concentrate)
- [[Groundnut oil]] | 2-3 tbsp
- [[Mustard]] | 2-3 spoons
- [[Onion]] | 1, cubed (not too small)
- [[Asafotida]] | 1 tsp
- [[Tiffin Sambar Powder]] | 2-2.5 spoons
- [[Curry leaves]] | dried, to garnish
- [[Cilantro]] | finely chopped, to garnish

## Directions
1. Wash 50g toor dal + 50g moong dal; add 2 cups water, 10-12 pearl onions, ~8 drumstick pieces and ½ tsp turmeric.
2. Pressure cook for 5 whistles.
3. In a pan, heat 1 tbsp ghee; add 1 cut tomato and sauté.
4. Add ½ spoon tamarind concentrate + a little water; cook the tomato in the tamarind water ~5 min.
5. Add the cooked dal mixture and salt (~1 tsp); bring to a boil.
6. Take off the flame; garnish with dried curry leaves and finely chopped cilantro.
7. Tadka: heat 2-3 tbsp groundnut oil; add 2-3 spoons mustard; add the cubed onion and sauté till golden.
8. Add 1 tsp asafoetida; turn off the flame and remove from heat.
9. Add 2-2.5 spoons tiffin sambar powder to the onions and mix — it cooks in the residual heat of the turned-off stove.
10. Cook 2 min, then add this to the sambar body and mix.
11. Return to a low flame and bring to a gentle boil.
12. Garnish with more fresh cilantro.
