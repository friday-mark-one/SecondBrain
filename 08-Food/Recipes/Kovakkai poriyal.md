## Ingredients
- [[Groundnut oil]] | 2 tsp
- [[Mustard]] | ½ tsp
- [[Channa dal]] | 1 tsp
- [[Asafotida]] | a pinch
- [[Kovakkai]] | 250 g, sliced into thin rounds
- [[Turmeric]] | ¼ tsp
- [[Curry powder]] | as required

## Directions
1. Slice the kovakkai into thin rounds (¼ inch); discard any hard/red seeds. Optional: soak in salted water for 10 min, then drain.
2. Heat groundnut oil in a pan. Add mustard seeds, channa dal, urad dal, asafoetida, and green or dry chili to temper; let the seeds splutter.
3. Add the kovakkai, turmeric, and salt. Sauté on medium for 2–3 min.
4. Cover and cook on low till tender and lightly browned, stirring occasionally (8–10 min). Uncover and fry 2 min to crisp.
5. Add curry powder and shredded coconut (if using); mix well and remove from heat.
