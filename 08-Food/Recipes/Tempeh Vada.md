Serves 2.5 · ~17g protein per serving

## Ingredients
- [[Tempeh]] | 1 pack (8 oz)
- [[Chilli flakes]] | 0.25 tsp
- [[Garlic powder]] | 0.25 tsp

## Directions
1. Bring water to a boil in a pan.
2. Crumble/cube the tempeh and add it to the water with 1 spoon salt.
3. Boil for 12–14 min.
4. Strain, then run the tempeh under cold water to cool.
5. Mix in 0.25 tsp salt, chilli flakes and garlic powder.
6. Air-fry at 385°F for 7 min, then a further 5 min.
