## Ingredients
- [[Ghee]] | for tadka
- [[Cumin seeds]] | 1 spoon
- [[Turmeric]] | to taste
- [[Green chilli]] | 3-4
- [[Onion]] | 1, cut
- [[Ginger garlic paste]] | 1 tbsp
- [[Tomato]] | 1, cut
- [[Tofu]] | 1 pack, crumbled
- [[Frozen peas]] | boiled
- [[Garam masala]] | 1 tsp
- [[Red chilli powder]] | ½ tsp
- [[Cilantro]] | to garnish

## Directions
1. Add ghee to a pan.
2. Add 1 spoon jeera, turmeric and 3-4 green chillies.
3. Add cut onion (1); sauté.
4. Add 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste; sauté till raw flavor goes away.
5. Add cut tomato (1).
6. Add salt.
7. Add crumbled tofu (1 pack).
8. Add boiled green peas (frozen).
9. Add garam masala (1 tsp) + red chilli powder (½ tsp).
10. Cook till the spices mix and the smell goes away.
11. Garnish with cilantro.
