## Ingredients
- [[Paneer]] | 250 g, soaked in warm salt water, cubed
- [[Onion]] | 1, chopped
- [[Tomato]] | 3-4, chopped
- [[Ginger]] | 1 inch
- [[Garlic]] | 3 cloves (or [[Ginger garlic paste]] 1 tbsp)
- [[Cashews]] | 5-7
- [[Salted butter]] | 1 tbsp + more for tempering
- [[Elaichi full]] | 2 pods
- [[Bay leaf]] | 1
- [[Cinnamon]] | 1 piece
- [[Red chilli powder]] | 1 spoon
- [[Garam masala]] | ½ spoon
- [[Sugar]] | ½ spoon
- [[Peas]] | boiled
- [[Kasuri methi]] | to finish

## Directions
1. Soak paneer in warm water with salt for 10-15 min; cube and keep soaked.
2. Chop 1 onion and 3-4 tomatoes; keep 1 inch ginger + 3 cloves garlic (or 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste) ready.
3. Melt 1 tbsp butter; sauté onion till translucent.
4. Add tomatoes, cashews 5-7, ginger and garlic; cook till tomato turns mushy. Let it cool.
5. Grind the sautéed mix, adding water as needed.
6. Butter in another pan; add 2 elaichi pods, 1 bay leaf, 1 piece cinnamon and sauté.
7. Add the ground paste.
8. Add salt, 1 spoon chilli powder, ½ spoon garam masala, ½ spoon sugar (balances tomato tang); cook 5 min.
9. Add the cut paneer.
10. Add boiled peas; cook 2 min.
11. Finish with kasuri methi.
