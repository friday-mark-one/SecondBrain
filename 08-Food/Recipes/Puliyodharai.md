## Ingredients
- [[Tamarind paste]] | 100 g, soaked 30 min
- [[Coriander seeds]] | 4 tbsp
- [[Channa dal]] | 3 tbsp
- [[Blanched peanuts]] | 100 g, peeled
- [[Curry leaves]] | 4 strips
- [[Mustard]] | 1 tbsp
- [[Turmeric]] | 1 tsp
- [[Asafotida]] | gooseberry size, solid
- [[Fenugreek seeds]] | 1½ tsp
- [[Kallu uppu]] | 2 tbsp (rock salt)
- [[Dry red chilli]] | 150 g
- [[Sesame oil]] | 150 ml
- [[Jaggery]] | ½ tsp
- [[Crystal sona masoori rice]] | cooked, to serve

## Directions
### Topping powder
1. Dry roast the coriander seeds and transfer to a plate.
2. Take ½ tsp of the fenugreek out of the 1½ tsp; dry roast till it starts popping, keep aside.
3. Dry roast 15 dry chillies.
4. Powder all of the above together and keep aside.

### Dry chilli powder
5. Add oil to the pan; add the remaining dry chillies and sauté. Cool down, then powder and keep ready.

### Puliyodharai paste
6. Add the sesame oil to a pan; once hot, add the asafoetida.
7. Add the mustard and let it pop.
8. Once the mustard pops, add the remaining fenugreek seeds; let them change colour.
9. Add the chana dal and fry 20-30 s.
10. Add the peanuts.
11. Add turmeric and let it fry in the oil.
12. Add curry leaves.
13. Add the tamarind paste.
14. Add the rock salt; keep the flame high and let it boil.
15. Once it boils, add the prepared dry chilli powder.
16. Add a pinch of jaggery (~½ tsp).
17. Once it thickens, add the topping powder and mix.

### Serving
18. Keep some extra powder ready to top freshly when serving.
19. Mix curry leaves with hot rice for better absorption.
20. Add the puliyodharai paste, mix well. Serve with vadaam.

## Notes
- Paste keeps 5-6 months refrigerated, about 1 month unrefrigerated.
