## Ingredients
- [[Crystal sona masoori rice]] | cooked
- [[Ginger]] | grated
- [[Mustard]] | for tadka
- [[Channa dal]] | for tadka
- [[Urad dal]] | for tadka
- [[Green chilli]] | for tadka
- [[Peppercorns]] | for tadka
- [[Asafotida]] | a pinch
- [[Cashews]] | fried
- [[Curd]] | to taste

## Directions
1. Cook rice.
2. Add grated ginger.
3. Temper with mustard, chana dal, urad dal, green chillies, peppercorns and asafotida.
4. Add fried cashews.
5. Add salt.
6. Add curd.
