## Snowboard

**Length**
- Should be between nose and chin when stood up
- 144-152 cm for weight 135 - 145 lbs

**Type**
- All-mountain
- Best for beginner to intermediate

**Camber and Rocker**
- Hybrid Camber

**Width**
- 245 - 255mm snowboard waist width for 8-9.5 boot size

**Shape**
- Directional twin

**Sidecut radius**
- Narrow better for shorter turn radius

**Flex**
- Medium flex (if not soft-medium flex)

**Base material**
- Extruded - since it is cheap and easy to maintain
- Sintered - expensive, needs frequent waxing

## Bindings

**Type**
- All-mountain

**Mechanism**
- Step-in or rear-entry

## Boots

**Boot flex**
- Medium to soft flex

**Riding style**
- All-mountain

**Lacing system**
- BOA

## Tips
- Go later during the day when the legs are swelling for the right boot fit
- Buy boots (new) -> bindings (used with caution) -> board (def used)
