Day 2 - 9/5/2026 - Saturday
Glenwood Springs, Aspen, Maroon Bells and Twin Lakes
--------------------------------------------------------
10:00 AM – 11:00 AM - Drive from Glenwood Springs
11:15 AM – 1:30 PM - Board the shuttle up to Maroon Bells. Do the 1-mile Maroon Lake Scenic Loop, walk the shoreline, take photos, and ride the shuttle back down to Aspen Highlands.
1:45 PM – 2:45 PM - Head into downtown Aspen and grab a quick sit-down lunch or coffee on the pedestrian mall.
3:00 PM – 4:15 PM - Walk to the base of the Silver Queen Gondola. Ride up (15 mins), spend ~30 mins enjoying the summit views of the Elk Mountains, and ride back down (15 mins).
4:30 PM – 6:00 PM - Drive East on CO-82 up over Independence Pass (~12,095 ft elevation). Stop for 15 minutes at the Continental Divide overlook.
6:30 PM – Sunset - Arrive at Twin Lakes along the lakeshore to watch the sunset over Mount Elbert.
Dinner on the way and stay at Colorado Springs

Day 3 - 9/6/2026 - Sunday
Colorado Springs area
-----------------------
From Colorado Springs, drive to Garden of the Gods. Do the flat 1.5-mile paved Perkins Central Garden Trail, photograph the red rock formations against Pikes Peak, and visit the Visitor Center.
Walk into historic downtown Manitou Springs for local shopping and lunch.
Drive 5 minutes to the Manitou Cliff Dwellings to explore the preserved Ancestral Puebloan cliff architecture and museum.
Head to Manitou Springs and board the Broadmoor Manitou & Pikes Peak Cog Railway. Ride up to the 14,115-foot summit, enjoy high-alpine panoramic views, and grab a famous high-altitude donut at the Summit Visitor Center. 
Stop by Helen Hunt Falls in North Cheyenne Cañon Park for a quick, zero-shuttle waterfall view from the bridge. 
Head into Old Colorado City or Downtown Colorado Springs for dinner, local craft drinks, and evening strolling on historic Main Street.
Dinner and stay around Colorado Springs
