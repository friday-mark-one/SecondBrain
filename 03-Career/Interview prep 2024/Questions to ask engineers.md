# Questions to ask engineers

> Questions to ask **engineers** (your interviewers / the team) — recruiter, hiring-manager and offer questions excluded.
> `[x]` = already asked/used. Struck items were crossed out.

## During the tech round — ask the interviewer *(Snap, 30 min Q&A)*
- Time spent putting out fires vs innovating or learning
- Quarterly performance cycle
- Teams present in same city vs distributed
- Career development opportunities
- ~~Walk through a typical day~~
- ~~Culture — break fast & move fast vs ensuring highest quality~~
- ~~Tech stack at Snap~~
- ~~Mobility across teams or orgs within Snap~~

## Team match — ask the engineering team *(Snap, 9/8)*
- How many team members and their tenure
- In-person vs remote engineers
- How many senior engineers and staff engineers
- Open positions
- Collab with other teams like product or ML
- 3 year roadmap
- Core features vs migration efforts
- Stale infra vs new infra
- Monolith → multi-cloud: what contribution
- Biggest challenges the team is tackling this year
- What will I work on if I join?
- Delivery quality vs taking shortcuts under deadline pressure
- Deep stack / tech stack and languages
- Oncall load / midnight wake-ups during oncall
- Work-life balance
- Growth into the next level
- Attrition rate

## Ask an engineer *(Airbnb LLM team — `03-Career/Airbnb/My LLM experience.md`)*
- 3 year roadmap
- What will I work on if I join?
- What tools for non-tech?
- What's the adoption of current tools?

## General tip
- Ask relevant questions to interviewers at the **end of every interview**. *(Airbnb review)*
